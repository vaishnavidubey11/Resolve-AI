# Product FAQ

## Warranty
All electronics carry a 12 month manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery. Warranty covers manufacturing defects and does not cover physical or water damage.

## Wireless Earbuds battery life
The Wireless Earbuds provide up to 8 hours of playback on a single charge and 24 hours with the charging case. Full charging takes about 90 minutes.

## Wireless Earbuds water resistance
The Wireless Earbuds have an IPX4 rating, which protects against sweat and light splashes. They are not suitable for swimming.

## Running Shoes sizing
Running Shoes are available in UK sizes 5 to 12. They run true to size; if you are between sizes we recommend choosing the larger size.

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, popular wallets and cash on delivery.

## Customer support hours
Our human support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM IST. We reply to email within one business day.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are delivered by email instantly and are valid for 12 months. They cannot be exchanged for cash.
