# Shipping and Delivery FAQ

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM IST on business days are dispatched the same day.

## Shipping costs
Standard delivery is free on orders above ₹999; otherwise it costs ₹79. Express delivery costs ₹199 on all orders.

## Where we deliver
We deliver to over 19,000 pin codes across India. We do not currently ship internationally.

## Tracking your order
Once your order is dispatched you receive an email and SMS with a tracking number. You can also ask this assistant to check your order status using your order number and checkout email.

## Delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the parcel is returned to our warehouse and you will be contacted to arrange re-delivery.

## Changing your address
The delivery address can be changed only before the order is dispatched. Contact support as soon as possible; we cannot change the address after dispatch.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available on orders up to ₹5,000. A ₹40 handling fee applies.
