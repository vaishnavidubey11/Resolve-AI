# Return Policy

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags attached.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, downloadable software, personalized products and perishable goods cannot be returned. Earbuds and other in-ear items can only be returned if the hygiene seal is unbroken.

## How to start a return
To start a return, go to My Orders, select the order and choose Request Return. You will receive a prepaid return label by email within 24 hours.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item was damaged or incorrect.

## Exchanges
We offer free exchanges for size or colour on clothing and footwear, subject to stock availability. Exchange requests must also be made within 30 days of delivery.

## Damaged or wrong items
If your item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact support within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund including shipping fees.

## Sale items
Items bought during sale events can be returned for store credit only, within 15 days of delivery.
